Detroit Lions · Report 002

How do we know Holmes wins?

We studied every completed Lions season from 2001–2025, then built separate evidence registers for quarterback decisions, drafts, major roster moves, coaching, elite player peaks and major adversity cases. Only after those modules were complete did we calculate the final score.

Show important construction rules

Team results: 70% winning percentage + 30% winning-season rate.

Quarterback: start-share, one-starter rate, year-to-year continuity and multi-year plan coverage. Interpretation separately identifies creation vs. inheritance vs. conversion.

Draft/development: same Report 001 blend: 65% mature draft index + 35% elite peak/homegrown-core index.

Roster resources: average move quality, positive-move rate and failure avoidance.

Coaching: continuity plus major decision quality. Dan Campbell is not falsely labeled a sole Holmes hire; the ownership-led organizational search is recorded explicitly.

Adversity: preparation, replacement execution, result preservation and adjustment. The injury itself is never graded as the front office's fault.

Does the verdict hold up?

Detroit is a Pareto-dominance case.

Holmes leads all seven scored dimensions. Because the final model uses nonnegative weights, any nonnegative weighted combination of these same seven category scores also leaves Holmes first.

What does 100% not mean?

It does not mean Holmes is “100% objectively correct.” It means the current seven evidence-coded scores are unusually robust to weighting changes. Different evidence, different scoring definitions, different era boundaries or future seasons can still change the model.

What should keep us cautious?

The Holmes era is clear—but unfinished.

No Super Bowl trip

Detroit reached the NFC Championship Game but did not reach a Super Bowl through the 2025 scoring cutoff.

2025 matters

The Lions regressed from 15–2 to 9–8 and missed the playoffs, preventing the active era from being treated as an invulnerable machine.

Three drafts are deliberately held back

2023–2025 already contain obvious contributors, but they remain excluded from mature class scores. The model refuses to grade young classes as finished products.

Public information only

We do not have Detroit's scouting board, medical records, ownership conversations, coach interviews or private contract negotiations.

Judgment remains visible

Draft, transaction, coaching and adversity grades include evidence-coded judgment. The formulas are explicit so readers can disagree with them.

The full workbook stays private

The organized 28-tab research database is retained privately. Public transparency comes from the evidence inventory, construction rules, source audit, exclusions and model stress tests.
