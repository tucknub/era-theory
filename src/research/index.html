How do we know?

Era Theory does not start with a score. We start with what happened, compare the whole eras, write down the rules before judging them, and then try to break our own answer.

How does the score work?

Seven football questions. Results matter most.

The public score is a weighted comparison of seven parts of running an NFL organization. The research category names are shown too, but you do not need the jargon to understand what each one asks.

Show the exact construction behind those categories

Team results: 70% winning percentage + 30% winning-season rate.

Postseason: playoff, division-title, deep-run, Super Bowl-trip and championship rates.

Quarterback: continuity, intended-starter share and multi-year succession stability.

Draft/development: 65% mature draft-class index + 35% player peak/homegrown-core index.

Roster resources: move quality, positive rate, failure avoidance and premium-resource hit rate.

Coaching: 40% continuity + 60% coaching-phase quality.

Adversity: preparedness, replacement execution, result preservation and adjustment.

Can someone else win?

Yes. Ballard wins a deliberately narrow model that only cares about drafting and roster moves. Grigson wins a narrow roster-plus-adversity model. That matters because it proves Polian is not hard-coded to win; the result changes when the definition of the job genuinely changes.

Why isn't the entire workbook public?

The organized 28-tab research database is kept private so the underlying research asset is not simply handed away. Public transparency comes from publishing the evidence counts, formulas, source policy, exclusions, limitations and a model readers can test themselves.

What can't this project know?

The model has limits, and we show them.

No private team information

We do not have scouting boards, medical files, ownership discussions, contract negotiations or internal coaching evaluations.

Recent drafts need time

The 2023–2025 classes are tracked but withheld from mature class scoring until enough NFL evidence exists.

Future draft capital is unresolved

The Sauce Gardner trade remains recorded but unscored until the on-field return and future first-round opportunity cost can be evaluated fairly.

2026 is still happening

The historical verdict stops after completed 2025 seasons. Active 2026 evidence does not move the published score yet.

Some categories require judgment

Draft, transaction, coaching and adversity grades include evidence-coded judgment. The rules are explicit so people can disagree with them.

300,000 tests are not a truth probability

The simulations tell us whether the ranking is fragile when priorities change. They do not mean Polian is “97.7% objectively correct.”
